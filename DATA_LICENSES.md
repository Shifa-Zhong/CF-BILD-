This is a mixed-provenance research compendium, not an assertion that every byte is MIT, CC0, or unrestricted CC BY.

## Reused experimental property records

Direct source: Zhong et al., Environmental Science & Technology Letters 2024, 11, 1193–1199, https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.estlett.4c00524. The source workbook https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.estlett.4c00524.s002 carries CC BY-NC 4.0 on ACS Figshare (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). Retain attribution and noncommercial terms for reused records. This scope includes raw copies, retained measurement tables, and source-derived structures/targets reproduced in audits, vocabulary, exclusions or evaluation tables. Curation and CV regrouping do not erase upstream rights. Upstream sources are NIST ILThermo (SRD 147) and Wang, Song and Zhou, Processes 2021, 9, 65 (https://doi.org/10.3390/pr9010065). No new measurements are claimed.
